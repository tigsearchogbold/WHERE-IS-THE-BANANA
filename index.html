System Architecture

Three-Stage Resolution Standard

  1. Confidential Intake

    Employees submit reports with optional identity masking. All metadata is stripped and encrypted before storage.

  2. AI Classification & Routing

    Automated text classification evaluates urgency, detects risk level, and assigns the ticket to the right resolver group.

  3. Transparent Closure

    Track every status update, reviewer note, and corrective action through a timestamped audit log until resolution.
